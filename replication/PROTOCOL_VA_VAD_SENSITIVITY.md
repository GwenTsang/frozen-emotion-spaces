# VA/VAD as an interpretable anchor space

Status: **prospective protocol; no result yet**
Date: 2026-08-16
Primary corpus: EmoTwiCS customer tweets
Primary geometry: `VA-2D`
Sensitivity geometry: `VAD-3D`

## Rationale

VA/VAD should be added as an interpretable anchor, not used to replace the
appraisal and frozen-encoder spaces. EmoTwiCS provides co-annotated emotion
labels, nine label clusters, and integer valence, arousal, and dominance scores
from 1 to 5.

The clusters are **not derived from VAD**. They are groupings of the 28 labels
informed by label co-occurrence, frequency, and inter-annotator agreement.
Nevertheless, category and VAD annotations are not independent: each emotion
annotation received its own VAD scores, and objective/neutral tweets receive
`(3,3,3)` by construction. Consequently, VAD-to-cluster prediction measures a
co-annotated relationship, not an independent validation of a categorical
theory.

VA is primary because annotation agreement was substantially higher for
valence (`alpha=0.633`) and arousal (`alpha=0.514`) than for dominance
(`alpha=0.192`). The clustered categorical target had `alpha=0.622`. The
source paper itself omits dominance from its two-dimensional visualization
because of the weak dominance agreement.

Evidence: Labat, Demeester, and Hoste’s EmoTwiCS corpus paper and the source
distributed with the official dataset, especially its annotation,
inter-annotator-agreement, and dimensional-visualization sections.

## Experiment V1 — decoding the dimensional annotations

Question: how much of the co-annotated affective geometry is linearly available
in frozen textual representations?

For each preserved conversation/duplicate-group-disjoint outer fold:

1. extract one locked frozen representation `H` without tuning the layer on
   the reported outer folds;
2. fit `H -> V`, `H -> A`, and `H -> D` on outer train only;
3. compare against intercept/ordinal-frequency, TF--IDF, lexicon-only, and
   random-encoder controls;
4. report MAE, Spearman correlation, and quadratic weighted kappa separately
   by dimension;
5. report VA jointly as the mean standardized error, while keeping dominance
   separate because its annotation reliability is low.

Both ordinal regression and ordinary ridge regression may be reported, but one
must be locked as primary before execution. Do not discretize hidden states or
select a Transformer layer on the same outer-OOF result later presented as
confirmatory.

## Experiment V2 — conditional information for emotion clusters

Compare, with the existing multilabel group-disjoint nested protocol:

1. `VA -> cluster labels`;
2. `VAD -> cluster labels` as a sensitivity;
3. `H -> cluster labels`;
4. `[VA;H] -> cluster labels`;
5. `[VAD;H] -> cluster labels` as a sensitivity.

Primary reporting must separate:

- all customer tweets;
- subjective tweets only;
- subjective, non-neutral tweets only.

The last two analyses prevent the automatic `neutral=(3,3,3)` rule from
dominating the apparent dimensional signal. Conclusions are limited to
conditional predictive information among co-annotated views. They cannot show
that clusters are generated by VAD or that VAD validates the clusters.

## Experiment V3 — Contrast and Representation

Repeat the counterfactual-site experiment in three capacity-controlled spaces:

- `VA_GOLD_2D`: standardized human VA scores;
- `VAD_GOLD_3D`: standardized human VAD scores, sensitivity only;
- `H_PCA2` and `H_PCA3`: dimension-matched frozen-state controls.

An additional bridge may use `VA_HAT_FROM_H`: cross-fitted VA predictions from
the model in V1. Every predicted coordinate for an outer-test item must come
from a model that did not train on that item or its conversation/duplicate
component.

Because scores are integers on a 1--5 grid, sites must be sampled from unique
coordinate vectors rather than merely distinct item IDs. Ties use one locked
deterministic rule, and the number of unique coordinates, cell rejections, and
tie frequency must be reported. No random jitter may be added in the primary
analysis.

Within each space, test whether greater Contrast and smaller Representation
distance predict held-out learnability. Compare standardized coefficient
directions and space-specific null percentiles; never compare raw Euclidean
sums across dimensions or spaces.

## Interpretation ceiling

These experiments can establish that:

- text encoders recover some co-annotated affective dimensions;
- VA/VAD contains cluster-predictive information beyond or overlapping `H`;
- design scores predict learnability in a low-dimensional affective anchor.

They cannot by themselves establish that VA/VAD is the uniquely correct
emotion space, that distances are behavioral similarity judgments, that the
clusters are natural concepts, or that emotion categories are reducible to
two or three dimensions.

## Compute allocation

- Frozen-state extraction: local CUDA GPU; do not prioritize the older Jetson.
- Linear/ordinal probes and geometry: CPU-only; the Ubuntu 14 GB SSH machine is
